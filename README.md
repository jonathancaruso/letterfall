# Letterfall

A falling-block word puzzle game. Letters fall from the top of an 8×14 grid; place them to form words horizontally or vertically. Tap valid words to clear them.

## Play

Open `public/index.html` in a browser, or visit the deployed version.

## Controls

- **Move:** Arrow keys, A/D, or drag on touch
- **Soft drop:** Down arrow, S, or swipe down
- **Hard drop:** Space
- **Clear word:** Click/tap any highlighted (yellow) letter
- **Pause:** P or Escape

## Stack

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Word list: ~25K curated common English words.
